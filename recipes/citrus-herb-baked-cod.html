Citrus Herb Baked Cod

Flaky cod roasted on lemon slices with butter and a bright herb crust.

Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Total
25 min

Ingredients

The spiceCitrus Herb BlendFresh Feels Forever

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and lay the lemon slices and asparagus on a sheet pan.
  2. Set the cod on the lemons.
  3. Mix butter, Citrus Herb Blend and panko; spoon over the fish.
  4. Bake 12–15 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
