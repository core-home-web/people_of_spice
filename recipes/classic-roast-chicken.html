Classic Sunday Roast Chicken

Golden skin, juicy meat and a pan of herby drippings for gravy.

Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
1 hr 15 min
Total
1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

The spicePoultry SeasoningThe Classic Roast Companion

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and pat the chicken dry.
  2. Mash butter with Poultry Seasoning and salt; rub under and over the skin.
  3. Stuff with lemon and onion; set in a roasting pan with the stock.
  4. Roast 65–75 minutes until the thigh reads 165°F.
  5. Rest 15 minutes; whisk the drippings into a quick gravy.
