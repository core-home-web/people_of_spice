Made with Parsley

Herb-Heavy Tabbouleh

The Lebanese way — mostly parsley, a little bulgur and plenty of lemon.

Serves
4
Prep
20 min
Cook
No-cook
Total
20 min

Ingredients

The spiceParsleyClean, Green, Easy

Method

  1. Soak the bulgur in the lemon juice with the dried Parsley for 15 minutes.
  2. Combine fresh parsley, tomatoes, green onions and mint.
  3. Add the bulgur and olive oil and toss well.
  4. Season with salt and serve with romaine leaves for scooping.
