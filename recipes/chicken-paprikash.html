Chicken Paprikash

Hungary's comfort classic — chicken braised in a velvety, brick-red paprika and sour cream sauce.

Mild
Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
40 min
Total
55 min

Ingredients

The spicePaprikaSweet, Red, Essential

Method

  1. Brown the seasoned chicken in butter; set aside.
  2. Cook the onions gently until soft and golden, 10 minutes.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the Paprika, then add the stock and chicken.
  4. Cover and simmer 25 minutes.
  5. Whisk sour cream with flour, stir in and simmer 2 minutes. Serve over noodles.
