Gingerbread Skillet Cookie

A chewy, molasses-dark cookie baked in one pan and served warm with ice cream.

Mild
Serves
8
Prep
15 min
Cook
22 min
Total
37 min

Ingredients

The spiceGingerbread SpiceWarm, Sweet, & Nostalgic

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a 10-inch skillet.
  2. Whisk butter, sugar, molasses and egg; stir in Gingerbread Spice.
  3. Fold in flour, baking soda and salt, then the chocolate chips.
  4. Press into the skillet and bake 20–22 minutes until set at the edges.
  5. Serve warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
