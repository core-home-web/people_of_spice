Chai Sugar Affogato

Vanilla ice cream, hot espresso and a crunchy shower of chai sugar — dessert in two minutes.

Serves
2
Prep
5 min
Cook
No-cook
Total
5 min

Ingredients

The spiceChai SugarSweet With Spice

Method

  1. Scoop the ice cream into two small glasses.
  2. Sprinkle with Chai Sugar.
  3. Pour the hot espresso over at the table.
  4. Top with crushed biscotti and eat straight away.
