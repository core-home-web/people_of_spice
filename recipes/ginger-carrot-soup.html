Silky Ginger Carrot Soup

Bright carrot soup with warm ginger and a swirl of coconut milk.

Mild
Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
30 min
Total
40 min

Ingredients

The spiceGround GingerWarmth with a Kick

Method

  1. Soften the onion in oil, then stir in Ground Ginger for 30 seconds.
  2. Add carrots and stock; simmer 25 minutes until tender.
  3. Blend until silky, then stir in the coconut milk.
  4. Season with salt and a squeeze of lime.
