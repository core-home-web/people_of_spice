Crackling Sunday Pork Shoulder

Fennel-and-sage pork with crackling worth fighting over, roasted on a bed of apples.

Mild
Serves
6
Prep
15 min
Cook
4 hr
Total
4 hr 15 min

Ingredients

The spiceSunday Roast Pork RubFrom Snout to Tail

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F. Rub the pork all over with oil and the rub, working it into the scores.
  2. Set on the apples and onions in a roasting pan and roast 30 minutes.
  3. Add the cider, lower to 325°F and roast 3–3½ hours until tender.
  4. Rest 20 minutes, then pull or slice with the crackling and pan apples.
