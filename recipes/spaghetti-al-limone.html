Made with Lemon Herb

Spaghetti al Limone

Amalfi's summer pasta — silky, lemony and on the table in 15 minutes.

Serves
4
Prep
5 min
Cook
12 min
Total
17 min

Ingredients

The spiceLemon HerbBright, Not Heavy

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti until al dente, adding peas for the last 2 minutes; reserve 1 cup water.
  2. Melt butter with Lemon Herb and lemon zest in a large pan.
  3. Add the pasta, lemon juice, Parmesan and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Toss vigorously until glossy; serve at once.
