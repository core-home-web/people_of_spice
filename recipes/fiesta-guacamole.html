Made with Hacienda Salsa Guacamole

Fiesta Guacamole

Creamy guacamole lifted with Hacienda Salsa Guacamole — zesty, fresh and made for dipping.

Warm
Serves
6
Prep
15 min
Cook
No-cook
Total
15 min

Ingredients

You'll need: Mixing bowl, Fork or masher, Measuring spoons

The spiceHacienda Salsa GuacamoleFiesta-Ready Seasoning

Method

  1. Halve the avocados, remove the pits and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  2. Mash with a fork, leaving it a little chunky.
  3. Stir in the lime juice and Hacienda Salsa Guacamole.
  4. Fold through the tomato, red onion, jalapeño and cilantro.
  5. Taste, adjust with more lime or seasoning, and serve right away with chips.
