Green Goddess Crunch Bowl

Cabbage, cucumber and herbs in a creamy Green Goddess yogurt dressing — crunchy, green and endlessly snackable.

Serves
2
Prep
15 min
Cook
No-cook
Total
15 min

Ingredients

The spiceGreen GoddessHerb-Forward, Always

Method

  1. Whisk yogurt, lemon juice and Green Goddess with a splash of water.
  2. Toss cabbage, cucumber and chives with the dressing.
  3. Top with avocado and pepitas.
  4. Eat as is, or scoop up with tortilla chips.
