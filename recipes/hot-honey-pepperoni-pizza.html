Chili Garlic Hot Honey Pizza

Crisp-cupped pepperoni, hot honey and a chili garlic crust — the slice-shop favourite at home.

Hot
Serves
2–3
Prep
15 min
Cook
12 min
Total
27 min

Ingredients

The spiceChili GarlicTurn Up the Heat

Method

  1. Heat the oven to its hottest setting with a pizza stone or steel inside.
  2. Stretch the dough and top with sauce, mozzarella and pepperoni.
  3. Brush the crust with olive oil and shake over half the Chili Garlic.
  4. Bake 10–12 minutes until blistered.
  5. Warm the honey with the remaining Chili Garlic and drizzle over.
