Quick Dill Pickles

Crunchy refrigerator pickles, ready by tomorrow's lunch.

Serves
1 jar
Prep
15 min
Cook
5 min
Total
24 hr 20 min

Ingredients

The spiceWhole DillPickle-Ready

Method

  1. Slice the cucumbers into spears and pack into a 1-quart jar with garlic.
  2. Bring vinegar, water, salt and sugar to a boil.
  3. Add Whole Dill and mustard seeds to the jar and pour over the hot brine.
  4. Cool, seal and refrigerate at least 24 hours.
