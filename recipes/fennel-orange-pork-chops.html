Fennel & Orange Pork Chops

Toasted fennel seed crust, juicy chops and a quick orange pan sauce.

Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Total
25 min

Ingredients

The spiceWhole FennelFragrant, Clean Finish

Method

  1. Rub the chops with crushed fennel, salt, pepper and orange zest.
  2. Sear in oil over medium-high heat, 4–5 minutes per side.
  3. Rest the chops; add orange juice and butter to the pan and swirl into a sauce.
  4. Spoon the sauce over the chops.
