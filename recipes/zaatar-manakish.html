Za'atar Manakish

Lebanon's breakfast flatbread — soft dough, olive oil and a thick layer of za'atar.

Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
10 min
Total
25 min

Ingredients

The spiceZa'atarEarthy, Bright, Timeless

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 475°F with a sheet pan inside.
  2. Divide the dough into 4 and stretch into rounds.
  3. Mix olive oil and Za'atar into a paste and spread over the dough, leaving a rim.
  4. Bake on the hot pan 8–10 minutes until golden.
  5. Serve warm with labneh and fresh vegetables.
