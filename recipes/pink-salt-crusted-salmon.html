Pink Salt & Citrus Salmon

Crisp-skinned salmon finished with freshly cracked pink salt and a squeeze of citrus.

Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Total
25 min

Ingredients

The spiceHimalayan Pink SaltPretty in Pink

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and pat the salmon very dry.
  2. Toss broccoli and tomatoes with half the oil and a grind of pink salt on a sheet pan; roast 5 minutes.
  3. Rub the salmon with the remaining oil, add to the pan skin-side up and roast 10 minutes.
  4. Finish with citrus zest and a generous grind of pink salt; serve with lemon wedges.
