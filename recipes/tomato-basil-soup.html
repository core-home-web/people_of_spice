Roasted Tomato Basil Soup

Slow-roasted tomatoes blended with basil and a little cream — grilled cheese not optional.

Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
45 min
Total
55 min

Ingredients

The spiceBasilA Fresh Finish

Method

  1. Roast tomatoes, onion and garlic with oil at 400°F for 40 minutes.
  2. Transfer to a pot with the stock and Basil; simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth and stir in the cream.
  4. Season and serve with grilled cheese.
