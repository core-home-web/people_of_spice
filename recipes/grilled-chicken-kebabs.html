Grilled Chicken Kebabs

Juicy kebabs marinated in Middle Eastern Manqual, grilled until smoky and tender.

Warm
Serves
4–6
Prep
20 min
Cook
15 min
Total
1 hr 35 min

Ingredients

You'll need: Grill or grill pan, Skewers, Mixing bowl

The spiceMiddle Eastern ManqualMiddle Eastern Grill Seasoning

Method

  1. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, garlic and Manqual in a bowl.
  2. Add the chicken, toss to coat and marinate for 1 hour.
  3. Heat the grill to medium-high.
  4. Thread chicken, onion and pepper onto skewers.
  5. Grill 12–15 minutes, turning occasionally, until charred and cooked through.
  6. Serve with warm flatbreads and yogurt sauce.
