Texas-Style Oven Ribs

Low-and-slow ribs with a peppery bark — no smoker required.

Warm
Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
3 hr
Total
3 hr 15 min

Ingredients

The spiceTexas BBQBackyard Approved

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 275°F. Remove the membrane from the ribs.
  2. Brush with mustard and coat generously with Texas BBQ.
  3. Wrap tightly in foil and bake 2½ hours.
  4. Unwrap, brush with sauce mixed with vinegar and broil 3–5 minutes until sticky.
