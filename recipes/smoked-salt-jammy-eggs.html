Smoked Salt Jammy Eggs on Toast

Six-and-a-half-minute eggs, buttered sourdough and a campfire finish of smoked salt.

Serves
2
Prep
5 min
Cook
7 min
Total
12 min

Ingredients

The spiceSmoked SaltWhere Fire Meets Flavor

Method

  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook exactly 6½ minutes, then plunge into ice water.
  2. Toast and butter the sourdough.
  3. Peel and halve the eggs; arrange on toast with the avocado.
  4. Grind over smoked salt, add chives and a drizzle of chili oil.
