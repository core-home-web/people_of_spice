Horiatiki Greek Salad

The taverna salad — juicy tomatoes, cucumber, olives and a slab of feta. No lettuce allowed.

Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
No-cook
Total
15 min

Ingredients

The spiceGreek StyleBright, Salty, Craveable

Method

  1. Arrange tomatoes, cucumber, onion, pepper and olives in a shallow bowl.
  2. Whisk oil, vinegar and Greek Style.
  3. Lay the feta on top and pour over the dressing.
  4. Finish with another pinch of seasoning and serve with crusty bread.
