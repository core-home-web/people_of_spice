Made with Spicy Chili Salt

Street-Cart Mango Cups

Mexico's favourite fruit-cart snack — mango, cucumber and jicama, lime and a fiery hit of chili salt.

Hot
Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
No-cook
Total
10 min

Ingredients

The spiceSpicy Chili SaltTurn Up the Heat

Method

  1. Stand the fruit and vegetable spears in cups.
  2. Squeeze over plenty of lime juice.
  3. Grind or shake Spicy Chili Salt over the top.
  4. Add a drizzle of chamoy if you like it extra tangy, and eat immediately.
