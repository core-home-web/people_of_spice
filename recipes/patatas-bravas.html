Made with Smoked Paprika

Patatas Bravas

Crispy potatoes, smoky pimentón sauce and garlic aioli — Madrid's favourite tapa.

Mild
Serves
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
40 min
Total
50 min

Ingredients

The spiceSmoked PaprikaInstant Smoky Depth

Method

  1. Roast the potatoes with oil and salt at 450°F for 35–40 minutes until crisp.
  2. Simmer tomatoes, garlic, cayenne and Smoked Paprika for 10 minutes.
  3. Spoon the bravas sauce over the potatoes.
  4. Finish with aioli and an extra dusting of pimentón.
