Sheet-Pan Chicken Shawarma

All the street-stall flavour, made on one pan — crisp-edged chicken, pickles and garlic sauce.

Mild
Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
30 min
Total
1 hr 15 min

Ingredients

The spiceShawarmaStreet Food Soul

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with Shawarma, oil and lemon; marinate 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 450°F and spread chicken and onion on a sheet pan.
  3. Roast 25–30 minutes until charred at the edges, then slice.
  4. Pile into warm pitas with garlic sauce, pickles and tomatoes.
