Grilled Caesar Wedges

Charred romaine, a punchy shake-and-whisk dressing and garlicky croutons — Tijuana's classic, sharpened.

Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
5 min
Total
20 min

Ingredients

The spiceCaesar-StyleA Classic, Sharpened

Method

  1. Whisk mayonnaise, lemon juice and Caesar-Style into a dressing.
  2. Brush the romaine with oil and grill cut-side down 2–3 minutes until charred.
  3. Drizzle with dressing.
  4. Finish with croutons, Parmesan and another shake of seasoning.
